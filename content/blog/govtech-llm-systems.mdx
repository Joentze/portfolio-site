At GovTech, I worked on a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system designed to serve over 1,000 users in a production government environment. My work spanned the full lifecycle: developing the pipeline, writing Pytest suites with over 90% coverage, and deploying on AWS Lambda and SQS.

Beyond infrastructure, I contributed to experimental work on vector embeddings using OpenSearch, enabling semantic search over large document sets. I also developed RESTful APIs to support SCIM user and group provisioning—an introduction to enterprise identity standards that reinforced how critical interoperability is in government systems.

What stood out most was the emphasis on robustness and observability. Whether it was analyzing 10,000+ chat messages via Python scripts or building a Next.js analytics dashboard, every feature needed to be explainable, auditable, and reliable. GovTech shaped how I think about AI systems not as demos, but as public infrastructure.

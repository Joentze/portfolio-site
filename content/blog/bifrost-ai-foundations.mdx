My first software engineering internship at Bifrost AI laid the foundation for how I approach frontend quality and engineering discipline today. I helped build the company’s first design system library—over 30 reusable components—using React and Storybook, with a strong emphasis on documentation and consistency.

I also worked on CI/CD pipelines that tested and deployed the library to a private NPM registry, and built GitLab test runners using Playwright for automated regression testing. This was my first exposure to treating UI components as production-grade artifacts rather than disposable code.

On the backend, I contributed to authentication, in-app credit disbursement, and user management APIs using Express and Prisma. Bifrost taught me how good tooling, testing, and documentation compound over time—and why engineering velocity is often a function of foundations laid early.

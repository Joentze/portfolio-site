During my internship at Voltade, I worked on a workflow management platform tailored for pallet manufacturing—an environment where software decisions directly affect physical operations. Using React, Hono, and Supabase, I helped build interfaces and backend logic that modeled real production stages rather than abstract CRUD flows.

One of the most valuable lessons came from implementing RBAC and ABAC directly at the PostgreSQL layer. Designing authorization rules that mapped cleanly to business constraints forced me to think beyond application-level checks and consider data integrity as a first-class concern.

I also explored WebContainer APIs to enable dynamic function creation, allowing forward-deployed engineers to focus on business logic instead of repeatedly reworking infrastructure code. This experience sharpened my appreciation for developer experience as a product requirement—not a nice-to-have. Overall, Voltade taught me how to build software that respects operational realities and scales with human workflows, not just users.
